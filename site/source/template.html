Mobile tire service FAQ

Do you really come to me?

Yes. Our mobile team installs, balances and repairs tires at your home, office or job site across Broward, Miami-Dade and Palm Beach counties. Request a quote and pick "mobile service at my location."

What can you do on site?

Tire installation, balancing, tire repair and oil changes. Alignments, brakes and suspension work happen at the Sunrise shop on Hunter Engineering equipment.

Can I pay over time?

Yes. Lease-to-own financing through Kafene covers tires, wheels and service. Apply online in a few minutes.
