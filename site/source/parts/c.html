Rebate how-to

How manufacturer rebates work

  1. Ask which rebates are activeManufacturers change their programs often. Tell us the tires you are considering and we will check what applies.
  2. Buy and keep your invoiceYour itemized invoice is usually the proof the manufacturer asks for. Keep the paper or a photo of it.
  3. Submit the manufacturer's formEach brand has its own claim form and deadline. We will point you to the current one. Confirm exact steps with Simon.
  4. Watch for your rewardThe manufacturer, not the shop, issues the rebate. Timing and format are set by the brand. Confirm wording with Simon.

Note for the team

Offers need dates and terms in text, not images

The old site showed 11 coupons as pictures: 2 house offers (an alignment with a tire purchase and an oil change special) and 9 manufacturer rebates. Pictures cannot be read by screen readers, searched by Google or updated without a designer. Here each offer is text with a visible expiry date, so it helps accessibility and SEO and is easy to change.

Questions

Financing FAQ

What can I use it for?

The shop offers Kafene for tires, wheels and service. Confirm exactly what is covered with Kafene or the team before you apply.

Does the shop set the terms?

No. Kafene sets the terms and makes the decision. We cannot promise approval or quote what an agreement will cost.

Will applying affect my credit?

We cannot say. Kafene's application and its disclosures explain what they check, so read them before you submit.

Can I get a quote first?

Yes. Request a quote and we will confirm pricing for your vehicle, then you can decide whether to apply.

Does it work for mobile service?

Ask us. Tell the team you plan to use Kafene when you book, and we will confirm how it works for your visit.

Who do I contact about my agreement?

Kafene manages your agreement, so contact them for payment or account questions. For anything about your tires or service, call us.
