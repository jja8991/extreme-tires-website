Fitment check

What we check before a wheel goes on

A wheel that looks right can still rub, miss the brakes or sit crooked. Here is what our team looks at. This is a general guide, not a guarantee for any specific wheel.

1 · Bolt pattern

The number of lugs and the circle they sit on, written like 5x114.3. It has to match the vehicle exactly.

2 · Offset

How far the wheel face sits from the centerline, in millimeters. It sets how far the wheel and tire stick out or tuck in.

3 · Clearance

Room for brake calipers, suspension parts and fenders at full turn and full suspension travel, especially with wider tires or a lift.

4 · Center bore and load

The center hole should fit the hub, and the wheel and tire should be rated for the vehicle's weight.

Questions

Wheel FAQ

Can I put any wheel on my vehicle?

No. The bolt pattern, offset, center bore and clearance all have to work with your vehicle. That is why our team checks fitment before ordering.

Do bigger wheels change my tires?

Yes. A larger wheel usually takes a tire with a shorter sidewall. The goal is to keep the overall tire diameter close to stock so the speedometer and gearing stay accurate.

Will my TPMS sensors work with new wheels?

The sensors can often be moved to the new wheels or replaced. Our team will go over the options when you ask for a quote.

Can I pay over time?
