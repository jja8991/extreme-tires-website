# Extreme Tires: Competitor Website Audit

**How this was researched:** WebFetch failed on every competitor domain (DNS `ENOTFOUND` for discounttire.com, tirerack.com, pepboys.com, lesschwab.com), so this audit is built **only from web-search results and snippets** of competitor pages and third-party listings. No live UX was observed, so page-speed, sticky-CTA and layout notes could not be checked and are marked (inferred) where they appear. Promotions found in search caches (dated 2025 to mid-2026) may have expired.

---

## 1. Competitor snapshots

**Discount Tire** (https://www.discounttire.com): Tire finder by vehicle, size, brand **and license plate**. The plate lookup skips year/make/model/trim; this comes from a hands-on review at https://therideshareguy.com/discount-tire-review/, and I found no official page confirming it. Guest checkout, with the install appointment picked during checkout. Its online page lists "upfront pricing, easy scheduling, 90-day satisfaction guarantee, free shipping to store." The "Low Price Promise" says it will match or beat a lower price, and the competing price must include installation; matches can be requested online or in store. Optional "Certificates for Repair, Refund or Replacement" can be added at purchase or up to 30 days later. They cover road hazards and pay a non-prorated refund within 3 years or above 3/32" tread (https://www.discounttire.com/certificates). Every purchase gets free rotations, rebalancing, air checks, flat repairs and inspections. Financing is the Discount Tire credit card, with "YOU DECIDE: $50 instant savings OR promotional financing." Per an Affirm release, Discount Tire and Tire Rack accept Affirm online. There is a fleet page with an inquiry form, a fleet portal, telematics and a fleet card (https://www.discounttire.com/fleet). "Treadwell" is its data-driven tire recommender (per trade press). Third-party listings show installs at about $49/tire in Tampa, covering mount/balance, valve stems, disposal and TPMS reset.

**Tire Rack + Tire Rack Mobile Installation** (https://www.tirerack.com/mobile-tire-installation?zip=33314): Coverage is checked by ZIP, and **South Florida is a listed market**. Mobile install is "from $40/tire" and takes about 15 minutes per tire. Customers can buy tires through Tire Rack or **book install of tires they already own**. The **assigned technician texts when en route**, and free re-balancing is offered within 30 days. In Fort Lauderdale ZIP installer pages, mobile visits carry a **$90 to $120 per-visit fee or minimum**, some listings cap wheel size, one notes EV-qualified techs, and hours are weekdays plus Saturday. Under the "Price Pledge," installers show an exact install price by aspect ratio, including optional fees. A "Top-Rated Installer" badge requires the Price Pledge plus a 4.5-star average from post-install email surveys (rated 1 to 10 on quality, courtesy and cleanliness). Product reviews come from about 260,000 consumer surveys covering about 3.48B miles. Customers schedule the shop appointment at checkout. Shipping is free over $50.

**Pep Boys** (https://www.pepboys.com/auto-care/tire/how-tire-install-works): Explains the install process on a "How tire install works" page. The site advertises a Price Match Guarantee. An optional Road Hazard plan covers potholes, nails and debris: a free repair, or replacement or pro-rated refund down to 2/32", non-transferable (https://pepboys.com/tires/road-hazard-protection). Financing is a Synchrony Car Care card: 6 months special financing on $199+, plus up to $100 statement credit on $500+ during promo windows. Tire size pages carry a "Special Financing Available" banner, and the nav has "Schedule Service." I found no Affirm.

**Firestone Complete Auto Care / Tires Plus** (https://www.firestonecompleteautocare.com/offers/tire-deals/): "Priced Right" price match refunds **200% of the difference** within 30 days against local ads. It offers an **on-time guarantee**: 10% off the next visit, up to $25, if tires aren't installed in the promised time. Customers can schedule install online or buy online and have tires held at a store. The "Triple Promise" is fixed right, priced right, done right on time. A **Lifetime alignment** option is offered. There are **city x service local landing pages** (e.g. https://local.firestonecompleteautocare.com/ohio/mason/alignment/). The banner reads "6-Months Promotional Financing & Save 5% on first purchase." Third-party listings show about $48.99/tire install, with a minimum of 4.

**Big O Tires** (https://www.bigotires.com): Pro-tec+ warranty "goes wherever you drive." Store credit card financing is 6 months on $199 to $1,199 and **12 months on $1,200+**. Online appointments are *request* style ("Request Appointment"), and each store has its own location page with local offers.

**Les Schwab** (https://www.lesschwab.com/free-tire-repair.html): The **warranty is included at no extra cost** for the life of the tire. It covers free flat repairs, and a covered road-hazard tire that can't be fixed is replaced free. It also includes free rotations, rebalancing and air checks, plus a 60-day satisfaction exchange. Customers can walk in or book online. Les Schwab credit shows instant pre-qualification at 18% APR (https://kiosk.lesschwab.com/credit). The FAQ sends customers to the local store for prices, so it does **not** lead with online pricing.

**Goodyear / Roll by Goodyear / Mobile Garage** (https://www.justtires.com/en_US/mobile-garage.html): "Pick Mobile Install" at checkout leads to a **date plus 2-hour window**, with **SMS and tracking alerts**. Customers are texted at arrival to show ID and hand over keys. The site checks whether the ZIP is eligible. Space requirements are stated: 5 ft on all sides and 10 ft vertical if covered. One page says mobile install costs nothing extra. The program was described as a pilot in older trade press.

**SimpleTire mobile marketplace** (https://simpletire.com/mobile-tire-installation): All-inclusive mobile price per installer, covering mount/balance, valve stems, disposal, TPMS reset and **12 months of 24/7 roadside assistance**. Customers pick an installer, time and payment online. Free shipping applies when Mobile Install is chosen. A **site-requirements checklist** asks for level paved ground, a 3 ft perimeter, keys and the wheel-lock key. There is a 4-tire minimum, and installer prices range from $45 to $160/tire.

**Tirebuyer / Tire Agent** (agirlsguidetocars.com review; worktruckonline.com): Tirebuyer connects about 3,500 shops and 150 vetted mobile installers. Tire Agent ships to home, FedEx or an installer, offers at-home install in some markets via GoMobile (2021), and advertises **no money down with weekly or bi-weekly payments** (lease-to-own style). I could not confirm whether these are still current.

**Mavis / Tire Kingdom, Sunrise** (https://www.mavis.com/locations/sunrise-fl/): This is the nearest chain competitor. It promises to "match or beat the best advertised price," refunds the difference **+5%** within 30 days, and offers **free brake, suspension, front-end, battery and exhaust checks**. Booking is online, and the Sunrise city page is SEO'd for the city.

**Local mobile-only competitors (Broward):** Mobile Fix a Flat (https://www.mobilefixaflat.com/), Oakland Tires Mobile (https://oaklandtiresmobile.com/), Jay's Mobile Tire Shop (https://jaysmobiletireshop.com/mobile-tire-repair-near-me/), Mobile Tire Service Broward (https://mobiletireservicebroward.com/) and SOS Mobile Tire (https://sosmobiletire.com/). All of them **lead with "24/7"** and list **Sunrise, Plantation and Davie by name** as service areas. Mobile Tire Service Broward has **per-service pages** (e.g. /service/tire-change/). None showed prices in the snippets. These are van-only operations: none has an alignment rack or wheel/lift capability (inferred from what they list).

**Local tire shops:** Tire Hut, Fort Lauderdale (tirehut.com) has about 500 reviews at 4.5★ on the Michelin locator, offers road hazard and custom wheels, and has pick-up/drop-off. Tire Country 4x4, Pompano (https://www.tirecountry4x4.com/) brands itself as the "One-Stop Shop for truck enthusiasts" for lift kits, wheels and accessories, and installs every kit it sells. **Note:** Michelin and BFGoodrich locators list a *separate* business, "Extreme Tires N Accessories," at 2071 SW 70th Ave, Davie (4.7★). That creates name confusion on Google, Maps and in AI answers.

**Custom wheel / offroad:** Fitment Dynamics, Medley (https://fitmentdynamics.com/lift-kit-installation-miami/) has **checkout options of Local Pickup or Installation**, posts **install time per kit** (e.g. 3h ±0.5h, alignment excluded), runs a SetMore booking calendar and publishes brand-specific SEO pages ("ReadyLIFT Lift Kits in Miami"). CustomOffsets (customwheeloffset.com, details from third-party descriptions) has a **fitment gallery of real customer builds filterable by year/make/model and suspension**. Each build shows wheel and tire size, offset and trimming. It also offers offset/backspacing calculators, "will it fit" tools, a visualizer, **Affirm financing** and free mount and balance on packages. 4 Wheel Parts: I could not verify its site features. Snippets mention only promotional financing and 40+ stores with ASE techs.

## 3. GAPS TO FILL (what the new prototype still lacks; biggest revenue impact first)

1. **Deposit or pay-online checkout for the quoted set of 4.** *Best:* Discount Tire guest checkout with install slot (https://www.discounttire.com); SimpleTire mobile checkout picks installer, time and payment (https://simpletire.com/mobile-tire-installation). *Why it converts:* a paid booking has far fewer no-shows than a form, and the customer commits while the price is on screen. It also lets Kafene approval run inline. *Effort:* **L**. A $25 to $50 deposit through Stripe or Square is an **M** first step.

2. **Instant SMS confirmation + "tech is on the way" text + 2-hour arrival window for mobile jobs.** *Best:* Roll by Goodyear has a date + 2-hr window, SMS and tracking (https://www.justtires.com/en_US/mobile-garage.html); Tire Rack Mobile has the technician text en route (https://www.tirerack.com/mobile-tire-installation). *Why:* the biggest worry with mobile service is "will they show?", and this is the proof it removes doubt. It also turns the slot picker into a real commitment. *Effort:* **M** (Twilio, or a booking tool like SetMore or Square Appointments with SMS).

3. **A written, branded warranty and free-services promise.** Examples: "Free flat repair + rotations for life of tires bought here," plus an optional road-hazard plan with clear terms. *Best:* Les Schwab includes the warranty at no cost, with free flat repair and free replacement if a covered tire is unrepairable (https://www.lesschwab.com/free-tire-repair.html); Discount Tire Certificates are non-prorated within 3 yrs (https://www.discounttire.com/certificates). *Why:* this is what lets the big chains charge full price without being undercut. Put it on every quote and it answers "why not buy online?" *Effort:* **S** (policy decision + one page + a badge on quotes).

4. **Price-match / "beat any written quote" promise.** *Best:* Firestone refunds 200% of the difference (https://www.firestonecompleteautocare.com/offers/tire-deals/); Mavis Sunrise refunds the difference +5% (https://www.mavis.com/locations/sunrise-fl/); Discount Tire's match must include installation. *Why:* Mavis, Firestone and Tires Plus all have stores within a few miles in Sunrise, so shoppers compare. A promise stops the tab-switch and fits a "text us their quote" CTA. *Effort:* **S**.

5. **Line-item "What's included" on every out-the-door price, plus a stated mobile trip fee.** List mount, balance, valve stems, TPMS reset, disposal, alignment check, and roadside if offered. *Best:* Tire Rack Price Pledge (exact install fees shown before choosing); SimpleTire all-inclusive with 12-mo roadside. *Why:* Tire Rack's Fort Lauderdale mobile installers charge **$90 to $120 per visit**. If Extreme's mobile fee is $0 or lower, say so in big type. *Effort:* **S**.

6. **Real service-area landing pages, not just city chips.** For example /mobile-tires-plantation, /mobile-tires-davie, /alignment-sunrise. *Best:* Firestone city x service pages (https://local.firestonecompleteautocare.com/ohio/mason/alignment/); Mobile Tire Service Broward per-service pages (https://mobiletireservicebroward.com/service/tire-change/). *Why:* every local mobile competitor names Sunrise, Plantation and Davie, and "mobile tire [city]" is a high-intent search. Chips on one page won't rank (inferred). *Effort:* **M** (8 to 12 pages with unique local copy, reviews and a map).

7. **Fitment gallery of real Extreme builds**, filterable by vehicle, with wheel/tire size, offset, lift height and price as built. *Best:* CustomOffsets gallery (filters by year/make/model and suspension; shows offset and trimming). *Why:* this is the top driver of high-ticket wheel and lift sales. It is proof the setup fits *your* truck, and it builds SEO (inferred). Extreme already has the builds; it just needs to photograph them. *Effort:* **M** (start with 20 builds in a JSON gallery).

8. **Lift/leveling kit pages with posted install time + "book install with purchase."** *Best:* Fitment Dynamics (per-kit install hours; checkout choice of pickup or install; brand pages like ReadyLIFT Miami). *Why:* lifts are Extreme's highest-margin job. Fitment Dynamics is in Medley and Tire Country is in Pompano, so central Broward is open. *Effort:* **M**.

9. **Second financing option (Affirm or Snap) next to Kafene, with "as low as $X/mo" on every package.** *Best:* CustomOffsets (Affirm); Tire Agent (weekly/bi-weekly no-money-down); Big O (12 months on $1,200+). *Why:* Kafene is lease-to-own. Prime buyers often prefer Affirm, and two options cover both credit tiers. *Effort:* **S**.

10. **Bring-your-own-tires mobile install booking.** *Best:* Tire Rack Mobile ("book an appointment for tires you already own"); local vans (Gaff's installs customer-purchased tires). *Why:* this captures the large online-tire-buyer segment that currently goes to Tire Rack's mobile partners. *Effort:* **S** (add a mode to the booking flow with a per-tire price).

11. **Mobile site-requirements checklist + wheel-size limits.** *Best:* SimpleTire (level paved ground, 3 ft perimeter, wheel-lock key); Goodyear (5 ft around, 10 ft vertical). *Why:* fewer failed jobs and fewer back-and-forth calls, and it signals professionalism. *Effort:* **S**.

12. **Fleet / commercial page + inquiry form.** Target Uber/Lyft drivers, landscapers, contractor vans and dealerships. *Best:* Discount Tire Fleet (form + Fleet Specialist callback + portal, https://www.discounttire.com/fleet). *Why:* mobile service is ideal for fleets because vehicles are serviced in their lot with no downtime. Fleet work brings recurring revenue. *Effort:* **S**.

13. **Satisfaction / on-time guarantee.** *Best:* Firestone on-time guarantee (10% off next visit up to $25); Discount Tire 90-day satisfaction; Les Schwab 60-day exchange. *Why:* cheap to offer, and it backs up "we show up on time." *Effort:* **S**.

14. **Review depth: Google reviews feed + post-job review request by SMS.** 27 reviews is thin next to Tire Hut's ~500. *Best:* Tire Rack automated post-install surveys feeding installer ratings. *Why:* review count is a local-pack ranking and trust factor (inferred). Automating the ask closes the gap fast. *Effort:* **S** (review link in completion text) to **M** (live widget).

15. **Keep the plate/vehicle/size tire finder from the old site** if the prototype dropped it. The prototype spec doesn't list it. *Best:* Discount Tire plate lookup. *Effort:* **S** to **M**.

16. **Free-checks hook.** Example: "Free tire, brake & alignment check with any visit." *Best:* Mavis Sunrise free brake, suspension, front-end, battery and exhaust checks. *Why:* a low-friction offer that brings people in for service and upsells. *Effort:* **S**.

17. **Optional "after-hours/emergency" lane** (decision for the owner). Every Broward mobile-only competitor leads with "24/7." If Extreme won't do 24/7, show clear same-day cutoff times ("Book by 2 PM for today"). If it adds even limited after-hours service, it competes for flat-tire emergencies. *Effort:* **S** for the copy, **L** for operations.

## 4. Where Extreme can beat the big guys

- **The only true shop + mobile hybrid in central Broward.** Broward's mobile competitors are van-only (no alignment rack), and the chains (Mavis, Firestone, Pep Boys, Tires Plus) are shop-only. *Show it:* a two-path hero ("We come to you" / "Visit our Sunrise shop on Hunter alignment") and a comparison strip of Extreme vs mobile-van vs chain store.
- **Undercut Tire Rack's mobile fees.** Tire Rack's Fort Lauderdale mobile installers list $90 to $120 per visit. *Show it:* "Mobile install: $0 trip fee on sets of 4 in [ZIPs]" (only if true), next to the ZIP checker.
- **Alignment after mobile install.** A van can't align, but Extreme can (Hunter). *Show it:* a mobile package with an "alignment at shop within 30 days, $X" add-on or a free alignment check, so the alignment upsell happens on the same order.
- **Custom wheels + lift under one roof, in the middle of Broward.** The nearest dedicated lift shops are Fitment Dynamics (Medley/Miami) and Tire Country (Pompano). *Show it:* a "Truck & Jeep builds" hub with a fitment gallery of local builds, posted install times, package pricing and a monthly payment estimate.
- **Higher rating, personal service.** 4.9★ vs Tire Hut 4.5★ and big-chain averages. *Show it:* name and photo of the tech or owner on the booking confirmation ("Your tech: ___"), review quotes that mention staff by name, and replies by text from a real person.
- **Text-first, bilingual South Florida service** (inferred opportunity; I saw no competitor offering Spanish/English WhatsApp). *Show it:* "Text or WhatsApp us a photo of your tire in English or Spanish" and a Spanish toggle on key pages.
- **Brand breadth with local stock.** Carries Michelin, BFG, Nitto, Toyo, Falken, Continental, Pirelli, Nexen and Cooper, and is listed in the Goodyear dealer locator. *Show it:* brand logo row plus "in stock in Sunrise, installed today" badges, which beat 1 to 3 day shipping from online retailers (inferred).
- **Fix the name confusion as an advantage.** A separate "Extreme Tires N Accessories" exists in Davie. *Show it:* use the consistent name "Extreme Tires & Mobile Tire Service, Sunrise" across the site, schema, GBP and dealer locators. Add a "We're at 7712 W Oakland Park Blvd" map callout and `sameAs` links so Google and AI answers don't merge the two businesses.
